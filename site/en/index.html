    Two pairs of hands, one rhythm

    Ivan’s own method, grown from the techniques of healers of the Eastern Mediterranean. The session plays like a score for two voices.

    IvanMarina

    In sync

    Both move as mirror images, at one tempo. The brain recognises the pattern, breathing slows, trust arrives.

    Out of sync

    The tempos drift apart: one hand slows, the other moves another way. The brain can no longer predict the touch and gives up control.

    Questions people ask

    Will it hurt?

    No, and that is a principle. Pain triggers defence: the muscle tightens even more. We work deep but precisely, and keep checking how you feel.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the treatment and length. Message us on WhatsApp or choose the consultation: it is free and we tell you the price right away.

    Ivan or Marina: who should I see?

    Back, shoulders, deep work, yoga massage: Ivan. Face, head, delicate work: Marina. Neck, legs, general therapeutic massage: either. Not sure? Choose “No preference” and we’ll match you.

    How many sessions will I need?

    An acute problem often eases in 1-3 sessions; a chronic one needs a course. After the first session we’ll tell you honestly.

    Which languages do you speak?

    Ukrainian, Russian, Spanish and English.

    “Breaking up crystals”, cracking joints: is it real?

    There are no crystals in the body to break by hand: what you feel is dense tissue and spasm. The crack during a correction is gas bubbles in the joint, not “a vertebra popping back”.

    What if I have an injury or a chronic condition?

    Tell us at the consultation. Massage does not replace medical treatment: with acute pain or an injury, see your doctor first and we will work within their advice.
