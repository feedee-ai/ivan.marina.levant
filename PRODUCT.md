# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS generated by a small Node script (`build.mjs`) into four language pages. No framework, no build dependencies — chosen so the owners can host it anywhere for free and edit prices/texts in one config file.

## Users

People in Valencia, mostly Ukrainian- and Russian-speaking expats plus Spanish and English speakers, looking for a massage that actually fixes something: chronic neck/back pain, headaches, recovery after load or injury, stress and insomnia, facial tension. They usually find the business through Instagram or Google Maps and decide on a phone in the evening.

## Product Purpose

Levant Massage by Marina & Ivan is a two-therapist massage studio (Carrer de Molina de Segura 2, metro Av. del Cid, daily 10:00–20:00). The site must turn a visit into a booking or a WhatsApp message. Success = a request sent via the booking form or a WhatsApp click.

## Positioning

- Signature Levantine four-hands massage by Ivan and Marina (synchronous/asynchronous work — "4 руки. Два розуми. Одна нервова система.").
- Work with the cause, not the symptom; "depth is precision, not force"; they explain what happens with the body.
- Ivan: shiatsu background from the Far East, Levantine techniques from the Middle East, manual correction, yoga/Thai massage.
- Marina: massage helped her walk again ~25 years ago; face/head complex massage, myofascial, neuro-reflex.
- Free consultation.

## Evidence

- Google: 5.0, 22 reviews (UA/RU/ES/EN).
- Instagram cases: Artem (circus artist; 1/3/5 sessions → 40 km bike marathon), violinist Marina (3 sessions in a week), recurring headaches (course).

## Brand commitments

- Visual world comes from their Instagram: dark studio with black curtains, graphite scrubs, warm skin tones; light greige plaques; tall condensed uppercase headlines; thin leader lines with dots; frosted pill plaques.
- Ukrainian is the default language; RU/ES/EN alternates.

## Open decisions

- Prices (owners will send). Session durations are assumptions to confirm.
- Production domain.
