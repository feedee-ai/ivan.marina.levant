# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site in `site/` (HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no framework). Pages are generated locally by `node tools/build.mjs` from `tools/data.mjs` (contacts, services, prices, reviews) and `tools/i18n/*.mjs` (texts), and the generated files are committed. Vercel serves `site/` with no build step.

## Users

People in Valencia, mostly Ukrainian- and Russian-speaking expats plus Spanish and English speakers, looking for a massage that actually fixes something: chronic neck/back pain, headaches, recovery after load or injury, stress and insomnia, facial tension. They find the studio through Instagram or Google Maps and decide on a phone, often in the evening.

## Product Purpose

Levant Massage by Marina & Ivan: a two-therapist studio, Carrer de Molina de Segura 2 (metro Av. del Cid), daily 10:00-20:00. The site turns a visit into a booking request in WhatsApp. Success = a WhatsApp message sent from the booking form, a hero slot, or a WhatsApp button.

## Positioning

- Signature Levantine four-hands massage by the duo (sync / async work, "the brain lets go of control").
- Work with the cause, not the symptom; depth is precision, not force; they explain what is happening.
- Ivan: shiatsu (Far East), Levantine techniques (Middle East), manual correction, yoga/Thai massage.
- Marina: massage helped her walk again ~25 years ago; face/head, myofascial, neuro-reflex.
- Free consultation; prices after consultation.

## Objections the site answers

Will it hurt (no: pain ≠ effect) · How much (free consultation, price on request) · Ivan or Marina (zone → who leads, FAQ) · How many sessions (cases counted in sessions) · Language (UA/RU/ES/EN) · Injury/chronic conditions (disclaimer, works within doctor's advice).

## Evidence

- Google: 5.0, 22 reviews (UA/RU/ES/EN); 16 quoted on the site in the original language.
- Instagram cases: Artem (1/3/5 sessions → 40 km ride), violinist Marina (3 sessions in a week), recurring headaches (course).

## Brand commitments

- Visual world: their own black-curtained studio as a stage, real photos only (crops from their Instagram).
- Ukrainian is the default language; RU/ES/EN alternates.
- Main CTA everywhere: choose a session → WhatsApp.
