The truth, from massage therapists

What most people won’t tell you. Tap a myth.

  • In reality

    There are no crystals in your body to crush by hand. What feels like “knots” is dense tissue, chronic spasm, and fascia and blood flow that don’t glide. Otherwise we’d use a hammer, not our fingers.

  • In reality

    It’s cavitation: gas bubbles bursting in the joint fluid, like opening a fizzy drink. The correction works with or without the sound.

  • In reality

    Pain is a protective signal. Too much pressure triggers spasm and the body resists. After good work, the body feels freer, not stiffer.

  • In reality

    It helps, yes. But self-stretching won’t release the deep restrictions your body has held for years.

  • In reality

    It’s work on muscles, fascia and the scalp aponeurosis. Beauty can’t be stuck on top of tension.

  • In reality

    If you have a specific issue — spasms, stiffness, headaches — you need a plan, not random visits.
